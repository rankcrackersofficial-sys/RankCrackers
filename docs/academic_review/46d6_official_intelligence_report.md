## 2. Source Year vs Target Year Law

To prevent temporal and cycle confusion across the codebase, four distinct year concepts are strictly separated:

1. **`missionTargetYear`**: The examination / admission target year that students are preparing for (e.g. `2027` for JEE Main & NEET UG; `2026` for RPSC First Grade).
2. **`sourceYear`**: The release year of the official bulletin, syllabus, or advertisement document (e.g. `2026` for NTA bulletins).
3. **`recruitmentSourceYear`**: The year recorded in the legacy backend recruitment record (`2026`).
4. **`studentCatalogYear`**: The year displayed in the student mission catalog (`2027`).

When `missionTargetYear = 2027` and `sourceYear = 2026`, the cycle relation is canonically tagged as:  
`cycleRelation = PRIOR_OFFICIAL_BASELINE`
