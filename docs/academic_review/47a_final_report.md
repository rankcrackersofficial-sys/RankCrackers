### Key Architectural Directives & Ground Truth

1. **Permanent Mission Identity Law Enforced:**
   All `proposedYearlessMissionKey` values across all 36 entities are strictly yearless (e.g. `bpsc_cce_prelims`, `uppsc_pcs_prelims`, `mpsc_rajyaseva`, `rpsc_ras_prelims`, `tnpsc_group1`, `appsc_group1`, `kpsc_kas_gp`). No recruitment cycle years are embedded in mission keys.

2. **Official Source Purity Law Enforced:**
   All 100% of URLs in the source registry and latest document registry originate from sovereign state or national government root domains (`.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, and verified statutory board portals). Zero coaching portals, aggregator blogs, or SEO lead pages were used or registered.

3. **Duplicate Ownership Principle:**
   Regional candidate interest does not equal state ownership. Union Territories without dedicated public service commissions (Delhi, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu) have their premier administrative cadres (DANICS/DANIPS) constitutionally conducted by the **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)**. Delhi Police Constable and Sub-Inspector exams are conducted by **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)**. Teacher eligibility in Goa, Chandigarh, and Union Territories is fulfilled by **CBSE CTET**. These are cleanly segregated to prevent duplicate mission creation in Firestore.

4. **Historical PYQ Archival Truth:**
   Official question bank archives across India exhibit wide variance:
   - **Gold Standard Dedicated Repositories (`OFFICIAL_EXACT_PYQ_DOCUMENT_VERIFIED`):** Kerala PSC (2014-2026 filterable database), MPSC Mizoram (2014-2026 multi-tier repository), OPSC Odisha (2017-2025 interactive filter), RPSC Rajasthan (2013-2026 master papers), MPPSC (2014-2025), CGPSC (2012-2025), GPSC Gujarat (2016-2025), MPSC Maharashtra (2014-2025).
   - **Listing / Candidate Corner Repositories (`OFFICIAL_ARCHIVE_LISTING_VERIFIED`):** UKPSC, UKSSSC, PPSC, JPSC, TNPSC, APPSC, TGPSC, A&N eRecruitment.
   - **Ephemeral / Window-Only Portals (`OFFICIAL_PORTAL_ONLY`):** Authorities like UPSSSC, UPPRPB, PSSSB, HSSC, JKSSB, APSC Assam, and KEA publish papers strictly during provisional objection windows.
   - **Strict Paper Collection (`NOT_FOUND_ON_OFFICIAL_SOURCE`):** Bihar CSBC and BPSSC collect physical question booklets post-examination and do not maintain public web archives.
