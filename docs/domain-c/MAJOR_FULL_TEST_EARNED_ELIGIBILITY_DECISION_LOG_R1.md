## 1. Endless mission law
The Crucible is not a four-orbit program. Four orbits are only a planning/visualization example.

For every active mission:
ORBIT_N = 1, 2, 3, ... without terminal N.
Each orbit = 98 days = 2352 hours.
Orbit N close = Orbit N+1 start.
Mission retirement/closure is the only lifecycle event that ends future orbit generation.

Student joining never starts a personal orbit. All learners observe the same mission-global clock.
Valid maturity, participation and assessment history persist across orbit boundaries and never reset merely because a new orbit starts.

## 2. Orbit-1 qualification
Orbit 1 builds Major maturity.
During Orbit-1 Phase 1-6, first-orbit learners use Micro/Macro assessment only.
The final Phase-7 Macro junction at the completion of the first 98 days is the FIRST POSSIBLE Major opportunity.

Major eligibility therefore requires:
- at least one complete 98-day mission orbit elapsed for that learner's governed participation history;
- final versioned Micro/Macro participation threshold;
- verified/taught content and paper readiness.
Calendar/account age alone is not sufficient.

## 3. Major opportunities after maturity
After Major maturity is earned, Major opportunities are attached to selected existing Macro junctions rather than an independent 30-day timer.

Planning junctions in every subsequent orbit:
- Phase-2 Macro;
- Phase-4 Macro;
- Phase-7 Macro.

The same pattern repeats in Orbit 2, 3, 4, 5, ... indefinitely while the mission is active.

## 4. Full maturity
Full Test requires a minimum of TWO complete 98-day orbits.
The FIRST POSSIBLE Full opportunity is the Orbit-2 final Phase-7 Macro junction.

Two-orbit time maturity alone is insufficient. Full also requires:
- final versioned participation gates;
- governed Major attempt/history requirement;
- 100% official syllabus/paper assessability;
- verified Full paper inventory/readiness;
- mission-specific official paper contract.

From Orbit 3 onward, a Full-qualified learner may receive Full at governed selected junctions under the same versioned rulebook. No artificial four-orbit ceiling exists.

## 5. Parallel lane law
At a governed higher-test junction:
FULL_ELIGIBLE && FULL_PAPER_READY -> Full.
else MAJOR_ELIGIBLE && MAJOR_PAPER_READY -> Major.
else -> Macro.

Macro/Major/Full are parallel eligibility lanes, not three sequential compulsory tests.

## 6. Test duration and geometry
Micro = 60 minutes fixed.
Macro = 120 minutes fixed.
Major = mission official exam/paper duration; geometry must be official-exam-grade and must not be artificially shrunk merely because it is called Major.
Full = exact official exam/paper/stage simulation with 100% assessable official content readiness.

For a mission with multiple official papers/stages, Full is a governed suite of those separate official papers/stages with their own official durations and geometry. Do not concatenate them into one artificial sitting unless the official examination itself does so.

## 7. Joining
Joining never changes the global clock.
For a 48h schedule beginning 10:00, join through 22:00 inclusive => current Micro eligible.
After 22:00 => current Micro skipped non-punitively.
Historical events are never recreated privately.

## 9. Supersession
Superseded:
- Major opportunities inside Orbit 1 before its final junction;
- Full eligibility after only one orbit;
- any four-orbit maximum;
- fixed Day-30/60/90 independent clock;
- 47h Rebuild + 1h Macro.
