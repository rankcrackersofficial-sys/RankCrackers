# DOMAIN-C — Major / Full Test Earned Eligibility — Founder Decision Log R1

Status: DISCUSSION DRAFT — NOT IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORITY YET
Date opened: 2026-10-12
Scope: Crucible progression gates that earn Test Arena Major Test and Full Test eligibility
Code mutation authorized by this log: NO

## Already locked Founder principles
- MAJOR TEST IS EARNED, NOT OPEN.
- A learner joining Crucible on Day 1 does not receive direct Major access.
- Eligibility must be earned through real Crucible tenure/progression plus governed assessment evidence; app-open/idle time alone cannot earn access.
- Major Test and Full Test are separate Test Arena products.
- Major is cumulative against verified + actually taught/eligible coverage available to that learner/mission at the earned milestone.
- Full Test is the complete official exam simulation and must follow the governed mission exam/paper contract.
- Micro and Macro remain formative Crucible assessments. Major and Full Test remain Test Arena assessments.
- 98-day orbit rollover must not erase a learner's earned progression history.
- Question/content readiness and authority health remain fail-closed gates; learner eligibility cannot make an unready paper launchable.

## Crucible temporal basis under current R1 discussion
- 1 phase/block = 14 days = 336 hours.
- 6 normal schedules per phase.
- Each normal schedule = 47h learning + 1h Micro.
- After Micro-6: 47h Rebuild + 1h Macro.
- No separate Recovery window.
- 7 phases = 98 days = 42 schedules + 42 Micros + 7 Rebuilds + 7 Macros.

## Open design question
Founder is now deciding after how many Crucible phases/evidence milestones a learner earns:
1. first Major Test eligibility;
2. subsequent cumulative Major eligibility;
3. Full Test eligibility.

The unlock must not be based on phase count alone. Candidate model under discussion:
ELIGIBILITY = minimum sovereign Crucible tenure
            + required completed/eligible Micro-Macro evidence
            + required taught-coverage milestone
            + remediation/integrity requirements
            + paper/content/authority readiness.

Exact phase thresholds and point/evidence thresholds are NOT LOCKED in this log yet.

## Candidate progression for discussion — not locked
A simple starting model to evaluate:
- Phases 1–2: Crucible-only foundation; no Major.
- After Phase 2 (Day 28): possible Major-1 gate, if evidence and coverage gates pass.
- After Phase 4 (Day 56): possible next cumulative Major gate.
- After Phase 6 (Day 84): possible next cumulative Major gate.
- After Phase 7 / Day 98: possible Full Test gate only if complete official exam coverage has actually been taught/verified and all Test Arena authority/content gates pass.

This 2/4/6/7 pattern is a discussion candidate, not a Founder decision.

## Design rule to preserve
Time is necessary but not sufficient. A learner must not earn Major/Full Test merely by waiting for Day 28/56/84/98. Conversely, a strong learner must not bypass the minimum Crucible tenure simply through points. Both time and evidence gates must pass.
