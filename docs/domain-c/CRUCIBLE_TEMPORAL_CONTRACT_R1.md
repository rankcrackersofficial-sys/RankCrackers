## 2. Normal 48-hour schedule
Every normal schedule is exactly:
- Learning: 47 hours.
- Micro: final 1 hour.
- Total: 48 hours.

Example:
- Day 1 10:00 -> Day 2 10:00 = first 24h.
- Day 2 10:00 -> Day 3 09:00 = next 23h; cumulative learning = 47h.
- Day 3 09:00-10:00 = Micro-1.
- Day 3 10:00 = next schedule begins immediately.

Six schedules = 6 x 48h = 288h.
