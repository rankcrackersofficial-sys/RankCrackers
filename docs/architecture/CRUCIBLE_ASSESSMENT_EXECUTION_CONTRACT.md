# RankCrackers — Crucible Assessment Execution Contract v1.0

## Purpose
Govern mastery-oriented adaptive practice inside the Founder-approved 98-day rotating Crucible cycle.

## Constitutional boundary
Crucible is not syllabus authority, not teaching authority and not a PYQ archive. It consumes only applicable verified + taught + knowledge-closed question authority.

## Eligibility intersection
Mission ∩ Effective Blueprint ∩ Current OSA/DSD ∩ applicable verified atoms ∩ valid Taught Authority ∩ QUESTION_KNOWLEDGE_CLOSED ∩ Crucible policy.

## 98-day cycle
Seven phases × fourteen days = 98 days; Day 98 wraps to Day 1. Micro/Macro remain Crucible constructs. Major Test is excluded and belongs to Test Arena. Smart Entry classifies Novice/Middle/Pro before cycle entry according to separately versioned policy.

## Question selection
Selection policy must be versioned and auditable. It may use mastery, recency, error patterns, difficulty, atom coverage and verified PYQ style signals. It may not select untaught knowledge, wrong-track content or use PYQ frequency to enlarge scope.

## No-PYQ behavior
Zero PYQ is valid. Crucible uses reviewed RankCrackers Question Bank items. Later PYQs may calibrate style/difficulty and add eligible items without restarting the learner cycle unless policy explicitly requires recalculation.

## Mastery events
Every attempt records questionVersion, atom/dependency set, policyVersion, response, correctness/scoring, timing where applicable, hints/explanations consumed and resulting mastery update. Regrading is versioned; history is not rewritten.

## Remediation
Failure may route learner to exact DigiBook Teaching Unit/atom remediation, then re-enter eligible practice. Remediation must not fabricate taught authority; completion must flow through Teaching Execution rules.

## Fail closed
No eligible questions → return coverage gap/remediation state, never silently widen scope. Invalidated upstream content/questions are removed from future selection while historical attempts remain auditable.

## Student UX
Show simple daily/phase progress, practice and remediation. Hide normalization and authority machinery.

## Acceptance tests
98-day wrap; seven 14-day phases; Major Test absent; Smart Entry version-bound; zero-PYQ mission runs; untaught atom blocked; wrong track blocked; no-question state fails closed; remediation links exact material; later PYQ does not reset mission; invalidated question stops future selection; attempt history remains immutable.
