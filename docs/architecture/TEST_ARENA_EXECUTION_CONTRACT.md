# RankCrackers — Test Arena Execution Contract v1.0

## Purpose
Govern exam-simulation and formal testing independently from Crucible mastery practice.

## Surfaces
Sectional Test; Full-Length Mock; Stage/Paper simulation; Historical Paper mode; Major Test. Additional forms require explicit policy versioning.

## Blueprint fidelity
Test definition binds Mission/Cycle context, blueprint version, stage/component/track, duration, sections, question counts/types, marks, negative marking, optionality, language and special scoring rules. Unknown official rules cannot be invented in an “official-pattern” test.

## Question eligibility
Current scored tests require applicable verified scope and policy-required Taught Authority plus Knowledge Closure. Historical Paper mode may faithfully preserve out-of-current-scope historical questions but must label historical scope and keep them separate from current mastery authority.

## Historical reconstruction
A historical paper is “official reconstruction” only to the extent exact official paper/event/appearance evidence is proven. Missing questions/keys/shift identity must be surfaced; RankCrackers answers remain nonofficial where final official answers are not established.

## Major Test
Major Test belongs here, not Crucible. Scheduling/scoring/eligibility policy is versioned independently.

## Scoring
Store raw responses and immutable test-definition version. Scoring engine applies the bound rule version. Revisions create regrade events; do not overwrite original evidence.

## Result model
Score, section/atom diagnostics, unanswered/incorrect/correct, time signals where captured, and confidence/coverage metadata. Analytics cannot claim precision unsupported by data.

## Fail closed
Blueprint ambiguity blocks official-pattern labeling; insufficient eligible questions blocks publication or explicitly downgrades test type—never pads with out-of-scope/untaught items.

## Acceptance tests
wrong blueprint blocked; negative marking exact; optional section rules respected; Major Test only here; zero-PYQ mock allowed from RankCrackers bank; incomplete historical paper labelled; unofficial answer not labelled official; current-scope test excludes historical-only knowledge; immutable response/scoring evidence retained.
