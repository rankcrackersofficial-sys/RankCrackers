## 1. Purpose
Revision Desk is the student's evidence-governed recovery workspace. It converts qualified learning debt into bounded, prioritized, explainable revision actions and preserves the evidence of recovery.

It answers:
1. What needs attention now?
2. Why is it here?
3. What exact taught knowledge/evidence does it refer to?
4. What should the student do next?
5. What evidence is sufficient to mark the debt recovered?
6. When should it be checked again?

Revision Desk is NOT a bookmark dump, generic to-do list, duplicate Crucible Rebuild, rank optimizer or independent mastery authority.

## 5. Revision candidate sources
A candidate may originate only from qualified, mission-bound evidence such as:
- DigiBook/Taught Authority: taught concept requiring governed recheck;
- Crucible: Micro/Macro/Rebuild weakness or retention evidence;
- Test Arena: question/knowledge/timing error evidence from formal assessment;
- PYQ Vault: incorrect/skipped/revisit historical question evidence;
- Rank Radar: a traceable performance driver or next-action recommendation, but only as a routing hint backed by underlying academic evidence;
- governed retention/mastery evidence.

Bookmarks alone may create a PERSONAL_REVISIT item but not an academic weakness claim.

Competitive rank/percentile alone may NEVER create academic debt.

## 6. Taught-content eligibility law
Before a scored/required recovery item is activated:
SYLLABUS VERIFIED
AND current mission/applicability valid
AND knowledge identity current
AND TAUGHT authority satisfied
AND source evidence valid
AND projection eligible.

Therefore:
SYLLABUS VERIFIED != DIGIBOOK EXISTS != TAUGHT != REVISION ELIGIBLE.

Untaught material cannot be labelled student weakness.
Historical-only PYQ may be revisited historically but cannot create current required debt unless independently within current taught scope.
Stale/withdrawn/superseded projections fail closed.

## 10. Priority constitution
Priority must be explainable and versioned.

## 11. Mastery authority
Revision Desk does not invent mastery.
A governed mastery service may consume qualified evidence and publish a versioned mastery assessment.

The existing Bayesian-style StudentMasteryEngine is PRESERVE_CANDIDATE because it:
- weights evidence/confidence/recency;
- requires minimum evidence;
- can fail closed to unknown.

Its current priors, half-life, weights and bands are NOT constitutionally frozen. They require later validation/versioned policy.

The legacy fixed +0.08/-0.12 mastery delta is NOT canonical.

Revision completion alone does not automatically increase mastery.
Hinted/read-only completion may resolve an action without proving mastery.
Mastery change requires independently qualified evidence.

## 12. Retention authority
A governed retention service may estimate retention from qualified historical evidence and must support UNKNOWN when evidence is insufficient.

The existing StudentRetentionEngine is PRESERVE_CANDIDATE for its evidence-weighted/fail-closed structure, but current 21-day half-life and 0.40/0.62/0.82 bands are policy-pending.

Legacy exponential decay with arbitrary stability/default 30 days and local DateTime.now is NOT canonical.

Estimated retention must be labelled estimated, not observed memory state.

## 13. Scheduling
Revision scheduling is support, not academic truth.

All authoritative timestamps use server/UTC-safe semantics.
Scheduling considers qualified retention state, prior recovery evidence, due history and policy.
Missed/late revisions do not automatically punish mastery.
Snooze/defer may be allowed within policy; required high-risk items remain explainable.
Queue workload must be bounded to avoid impossible backlogs.

The existing schedule-support engine is PRESERVE_CANDIDATE structurally, but its 12h/1d/3d/7d intervals are policy-pending.
Legacy local-clock fixed interval scheduler is not canonical.

## 14. Completion and resolution
Opening, reading or spending time is not sufficient recovery evidence by itself.

Resolution may require, depending on debt type:
- successful governed retrieval/practice;
- corrected question/dependency evidence;
- validated recheck;
- completion of required relearning plus later retrieval evidence;
- explicit personal dismissal only for PERSONAL_REVISIT.

A required academic debt cannot be silently dismissed as mastered.
Resolution never rewrites historical source attempts.
Duplicate completion events are idempotent.
A resolved debt may reopen after new qualified failure, retention risk, regrade, content/version change or scheduled recheck.

## 15. Rebuild boundary
Crucible Rebuild is a governed recovery event inside the Crucible learning/assessment rhythm.
Revision Desk is the persistent cross-source debt orchestration surface.

Revision Desk may:
- feed eligible debt into a Crucible Rebuild;
- display Rebuild-derived recovery evidence;
- preserve unresolved debt beyond a Rebuild window.

Revision Desk may NOT:
- grant Crucible eligibility;
- alter Crucible day/phase;
- mark Day13 Rebuild complete by itself;
- duplicate a second independent Rebuild authority.

Standalone legacy RebuildEngine must be reconciled under Crucible/Revision ownership before runtime use.

## 16. Crucible 98-day law
Day98 -> Day1 rollover never deletes Revision Desk debt/history.
Revision debt follows student academic evidence, not cycle numbering.
Cycle identity may remain provenance on source evidence.
Unresolved debt persists or is superseded only by governed evidence/policy, never by rollover.

## 17. Test Arena boundary
Test Arena remains immutable formal assessment truth.
Revision Desk may consume qualified:
incorrect/omitted question evidence,
knowledge-dependency gaps,
timing/execution evidence,
section/subject diagnostics.

It does not modify historical Test Arena scores, attempts, ranks or percentiles.
Formal rank/percentile is contextual competitive evidence, not itself a weakness.
Regrade/revised scoring can supersede affected debt through a new auditable event.

## 18. PYQ Vault boundary
PYQ Vault owns official historical question provenance and learner PYQ state.
Revision Desk may consume an eligible PYQ revisit/error candidate while preserving QuestionAppearance, CanonicalQuestionVersion, official source and answer-authority version.
It must never copy a PYQ into provenance-free revision text.
Historical-only PYQ cannot create required current-scope debt.

## 19. Rank Radar boundary
Rank Radar owns competitive interpretation and may recommend a Revision Desk action.
Every Radar-originated action must resolve to underlying qualified academic evidence.
Revision Desk completion cannot directly increase rank/percentile or manufacture competitive trajectory.
No circular "Radar says weak -> revise -> Radar score rises" loop without new independent evidence.

## 20. DigiBook boundary
DigiBook/Taught Authority owns taught material and taught truth.
Revision Desk can deep-link to the exact eligible DigiBook/knowledge material for relearning.
Opening DigiBook does not automatically resolve debt.
If taught content/version is invalidated, affected debt is blocked/rebased through governed supersession.

## 21. Question Repository boundary
Question-based recovery references canonical QuestionID/version and knowledge dependency; it does not duplicate mutable question text as authority.
Question correction, answer correction, solution revision or mapping change triggers bounded re-evaluation/supersession of affected debt.

## 22. Student UX
Canonical surface should emphasize recovery, not failure.

Recommended sections:
TODAY — bounded highest-value actions
WHY THIS — source/reason/evidence
RELEARN — exact taught reference where needed
RETRIEVE/PRACTICE — governed recovery action
LATER — scheduled maintenance/recheck
RECOVERED — auditable progress/history

Avoid:
"weak student",
alarmist "critical rebuild required" language,
fake retention precision,
unbounded red backlog,
compulsive streak pressure,
selection/rank promises.

Student must understand why an item exists and what evidence will clear it.

## 23. Backend/security
Authoritative debt/priority/mastery/retention calculations are server/governed-domain outputs, not student-authored truth.
Students may submit permitted interaction/attempt evidence but cannot author:
mastery,
retention probability,
required debt,
priority authority,
source lineage,
Taught status,
resolution authority.

Every write validates StudentID, MissionID, knowledge/version/projection and source identity.
Use server/UTC time.
Offline replay is idempotent and version-aware.

## 24. Cross-mission safety
Revision state is mission-bound.
Same topic label across missions never establishes identity.
Cross-mission reuse requires explicit canonical applicability and current taught eligibility.
NEET evidence cannot silently create JEE debt, or vice versa.
Mission switch does not delete old history; it changes active projection.
