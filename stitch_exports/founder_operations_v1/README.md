## 2. Zero Manual Review & Autonomous Architecture

- **Zero Micro Verification:** The Founder does NOT verify syllabus atoms, questions, Crucible tests, DigiBook units, or routine facts. There are **zero** "Approve Atom" or routine approval buttons.
- **Autonomous Dual-Worker Consensus:** Every artifact is independently generated by Worker 1 and verified by Worker 2.
- **Decision Policy Matrix:**
  - `AUTO-PASS`: Dual-worker consensus (>90% confidence) -> Automatic release to student delivery.
  - `AUTO-REPAIR`: Minor deterministic schema/whitespace normalization.
  - `AUTO-HOLD`: Systemic contradiction -> Held at Gate 12 for grouped Founder policy override.
- **Founder Executive Governance Authority:**
  - `[Emergency Halt Pipeline]`
  - `[Halt Student Delivery]`
  - `[Archive Mission]`
  - `[System Policy Settings]`
  - `[Apply Policy Override: Dual Answer Acceptance Rule POL-902]` (Systemic rule injection only)

## 3. Real Backend Contract (Firestore)

- **Collections:**
  - `automation_runs`
  - `automation_exceptions`
- **Supported Statuses:** `queued`, `generating`, `verifying`, `reconciling`, `auto_passed`, `auto_repaired`, `auto_hold`, `failed`.
- **Zero Fabricated Metrics:** No fake revenue or mock candidate counters. Fallback states show `Awaiting live sync` or `Demo data (Simulation Mode)`.
